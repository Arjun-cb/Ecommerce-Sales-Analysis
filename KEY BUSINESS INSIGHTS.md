KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. Technology category is performing very well because both sales and profits are high.

2. Furniture category has good sales but very low profitability.

3. Central region is generating negative profit in the Furniture category.

4. High sales customers are not always high profit customers.

5. Discounts may be reducing profitability in some categories and regions.

6. Some products are consistently generating losses for the business.

7. Monthly sales and profits fluctuate over time, showing changing business performance trends.

8. Top profitable customers contribute significantly to overall business growth.
