# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing E-Commerce sales, profit, customer behavior, and regional performance using SQL and Power BI. The main goal of this project is to identify business problems, understand profitability trends, and provide business recommendations through data analysis and visualization.

---

## Tools Used

* MySQL
* Power BI
* SQL
* GitHub

---

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify high-performing and low-performing categories
* Understand regional profitability
* Analyze customer profitability
* Study discount impact on profits
* Track monthly sales trends

---

## KPI Metrics

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Average Sales

---

## Key Analysis Performed

### Category Analysis

* Compared sales and profits across categories
* Identified Furniture category with low profitability

### Regional Analysis

* Identified best and worst performing regions
* Found Central region generating losses in Furniture category

### Customer Analysis

* Identified top profitable customers
* Compared high sales customers vs high profit customers

### Product Analysis

* Identified highest selling and loss-making products

### Time Intelligence Analysis

* Analyzed monthly sales trends and profit trends

---

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive slicers
* KPI cards
* Category analysis visuals
* Regional profitability analysis
* Customer intelligence charts
* Monthly trend analysis

---

## Key Business Insights

* Technology category performs strongly in both sales and profits
* Furniture category has high sales but low profits
* Central region negatively impacts Furniture profitability
* High sales customers are not always high profit customers
* Discounts may affect profitability in certain categories

---

## Business Recommendations

* Reduce excessive discounts in Furniture category
* Investigate losses in Central region
* Focus more on profitable customers and products
* Improve profit margins instead of only increasing sales

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to solve real-world business problems through data analysis, dashboard visualization, and business storytelling.
